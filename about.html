mail-sorter

About & privacy policy · last updated November 24, 2026

mail-sorter is a private, single-family tool. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It runs on the family's own computer and helps sort the family's own Gmail and summarize school emails for the family.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission (Google sign-in), it reads, labels and archives messages in that owner's own Gmail account. It does not send email and does not access any other Google data.

How data is used and stored

Email content is processed on the family's own computer. Results are kept on that computer, and a family summary page is published only in encrypted form that requires a family passcode to open. No email data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google user data

mail-sorter's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The account owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored data from the family computer.
